# Tropical staples · step 1

Future-catalog candidates from the [curated expansion list](../../../docs/data/usda.md). The three subjects have no current item record. A search of the manifest, requests, staging, live masters, and exports found no duplicate illustrations for these subjects before generation. All proposed asset IDs below are provisional.

Orchard style references are the [illustration-language study](../../../docs/design/references/04-illustration-language.png) and [approved item apple](../../masters/apples-thumbnail-v2.png). Intended display width is 96–112 CSS px.

Candidates await Jared's review.

## Bananas

Proposed asset ID `provisional-bananas-thumbnail-v1`.

The Dwarf Cavendish descriptor row records slightly curved fruit, slight ridging, and a blunt tip. [ICAR fruit morphology study, Table 2](https://krishikosh.egranth.ac.in/server/api/core/bitstreams/af6cb871-17b8-4e4c-a4e9-f9ec12e2640c/content). UF/IFAS documents the arrangement of attached fruits in hands. [UF/IFAS, Banana Growing in the Florida Home Landscape](https://edis.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/MG040/pdf).

> Create one isolated Orchard produce thumbnail as a square PNG at least 1024 pixels per side with genuine transparent alpha. The first reference is a style sheet for paint handling, and the second is an approved apple for the level of detail and isolation. Paint a naturalistic botanical watercolor with luminous layered washes, broad shapes that follow the volume, restrained granulation, occasional pigment pooling, and a few softly broken edges. Choose a gently elevated three-quarter view and diffuse upper-left light. Center the subject within clear padding of about 10% on all sides and make the silhouette readable at 96–112 pixels. Keep pigment and texture inside the subject. Exclude foliage, cut fruit, ground shadows, paper mats, checkerboards, text, borders, heavy outlines, and plastic gloss. A compact cluster of three whole ripe Dwarf Cavendish dessert bananas attached at one short cut crown. Each finger has a smooth bowed arc, a plump rounded profile with only subtle shallow longitudinal ribs, and a blunt dark blossom tip. Let the three forms fan out slightly and overlap naturally, with varied curves and all three tips visible. Use warm golden yellow with a little muted green at the crown and very sparse freckles. Read as a compact, rounded, bowed group even in silhouette.

**Check status** · [Staged PNG](provisional-bananas-thumbnail-v1.png). The three blunt tips, shared cut crown and rounded, bowed fingers read clearly at 106 px.

## Plantains

Proposed asset ID `provisional-plantains-thumbnail-v1`.

The French Horn accession NYO0241 is documented with long fruit, a pronounced curve and ridges, and a lengthily pointed apex. Green depicts the unripe stage. [INIBAP Musa germplasm catalogue](https://cgspace.cgiar.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/2ba3c56b-e9ad-4ea8-af61-44214dc3b553/content).

Paint a naturalistic botanical watercolor with luminous layered washes, broad shapes that follow the volume, restrained granulation, occasional pigment pooling, and a few softly broken edges. Choose a gently elevated three-quarter view and diffuse upper-left light. Center the subject within clear padding of about 10% on all sides and make the silhouette readable at 96–112 pixels. Keep pigment and texture inside the subject. Exclude foliage, cut fruit, ground shadows, paper mats, checkerboards, text, borders, heavy outlines, and plastic gloss. Exactly one whole green French Horn–type cooking plantain. Show the full length diagonally with a robust cut stalk at one end and a long taper to a pointed dark floral remnant at the other. Give it an elongated body with a gently arcing midsection and a stronger bend toward the pointed end, broad planar sides, and two or three pronounced longitudinal ridge breaks that clearly describe its angular cross section. Use natural muted green with yellow-green lit planes, olive shadows, and a few small brown handling marks. The long, faceted, tapered silhouette must be recognizable without its green color.

**Check status** · [Staged PNG](provisional-plantains-thumbnail-v1.png). The long, faceted body, pronounced peel ridges and pointed blossom end remain visible at 106 px.
