# Citrus and orchard fruit staging

Future-catalog thumbnails from the [curated USDA expansion list](../../../docs/data/usda.md). Step one covers lemons, limes, and Asian pears. Step two covers cranberries and dates. All five IDs are provisional because the subjects have no catalog records. No matching assets or requests were found in the existing art inventory before generation.

Style starts with [Orchard illustration language](../../../docs/design/references/04-illustration-language.png) and the [isolated produce guide](../../../docs/design/illustration-guide.md#A-isolated-produce-illustration). Each candidate targets 96–112 px display and will be checked at that size against ivory and warm charcoal. Jared makes the review decision.

## Lemons

**Provisional ID** `provisional-lemons-thumbnail-v1`

**Verified anatomy** Eureka lemon has a yellow elliptical to oblong fruit, usually a short apical nipple and finely pitted rind. [UC Riverside Citrus Variety Collection](https://citrusvariety.ucr.edu/crc3005).

**Staged style reference** [oranges-thumbnail-v2.png](../03-oranges-grapefruit/oranges-thumbnail-v2.png).

**Prompt**

> Create a single Orchard item thumbnail in naturalistic botanical watercolor for a contemporary illustrated produce journal. The first reference is the Orchard style sheet. Use its translucent layered washes, gentle granulation and softly varied edges. Use the second reference for specimen scale, brush finish and upper-left diffuse lighting. Both references are style only. Use a gently elevated three-quarter view. Keep a clear silhouette and restrained fine detail that reads at 96–112 px. Center the subject with generous clear margins on every side. Deliver square PNG artwork at least 1024 × 1024 with genuine transparent alpha outside the fruit. No paper patch, painted checkerboard, halo, ground shadow, border, stickers, lettering, heavy outlines or plastic gloss. Paint one whole ripe Eureka lemon without foliage or a cut piece. Set its oblong axis at a gentle diagonal, with a small natural apical nipple at one end, a subtle stem scar at the other, and a bright yellow, finely pitted rind. Use a pale lemon-yellow wash on the lit shoulder, warm yellow midtones and a restrained ochre shadow. Keep the nipple small and the oil glands selectively visible.

**Check status** [Candidate PNG](provisional-lemons-thumbnail-v1.png) generated with the built-in tool. `checkMaster` passed on the 1254 × 1254 transparent PNG. Pass at 104 px on ivory and warm charcoal. The oblong silhouette, apical nipple, yellow rind and pitting read clearly. Approved by Jared.

## Limes

**Provisional ID** `provisional-limes-thumbnail-v1`

**Verified anatomy** Persian/Tahiti lime has an oval fruit and relatively smooth rind. Render the familiar green market stage. The flesh is pale greenish yellow, finely textured and typically seedless. [UF/IFAS market and fruit description](https://edis.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/FE1122/pdf) and [UC Riverside Bearss accession](https://citrusvariety.ucr.edu/crc3772).

**Staged style reference** [oranges-thumbnail-v2.png](../03-oranges-grapefruit/oranges-thumbnail-v2.png).

**Prompt**

> Create a single Orchard item thumbnail in naturalistic botanical watercolor for a contemporary illustrated produce journal. The first reference is the Orchard style sheet. Use its translucent layered washes, gentle granulation and softly varied edges. Use the second reference for specimen scale, brush finish and upper-left diffuse lighting. Both references are style only. Use a gently elevated three-quarter view. Keep a clear silhouette and restrained fine detail that reads at 96–112 px. Center the subject with generous clear margins on every side. Deliver square PNG artwork at least 1024 × 1024 with genuine transparent alpha outside the fruit. No paper patch, painted checkerboard, halo, ground shadow, border, stickers, lettering, heavy outlines or plastic gloss. Paint one whole green Persian (Bearss/Tahiti) lime with one closely overlapping small cut wedge, following the staged orange grouping. The whole fruit is gently oval with rounded ends and a thin, comparatively smooth rind with subtle oil pores. Use natural fresh leaf-green midtones, a yellow-green upper-left wash and deeper forest-green shade. Angle the cut face toward the viewer. Show pale greenish-yellow seedless flesh, radial segment membranes and thin pale pith. Keep the cut piece subordinate and omit foliage.

**Check status** [Candidate PNG](provisional-limes-thumbnail-v1.png) generated with the built-in tool. `checkMaster` passed on the 1254 × 1254 transparent PNG. Pass at 104 px on ivory and warm charcoal. The green oval fruit and pale segmented wedge read clearly. The rind pores are slightly prominent at master scale. Approved by Jared.

**Provisional ID** `provisional-asian-pears-thumbnail-v1`

**Verified anatomy** Hosui is a round Asian pear with golden-brown russeted skin and conspicuous pale lenticels. [Washington State University Tree Fruit](https://treefruit.wsu.edu/variety/hosui-sweet-water/) and [NSW Agriculture cultivar profile](https://www.dpird.nsw.gov.au/agriculture/horticulture/pomes/apples/nashi/hosui).

**Staged style reference** [pears-bosc-thumbnail-v2.png](../04-pears/pears-bosc-thumbnail-v2.png).

**Prompt**

> Create a single Orchard item thumbnail in naturalistic botanical watercolor for a contemporary illustrated produce journal. The first reference is the Orchard style sheet. Use its translucent layered washes, gentle granulation and softly varied edges. Use the second reference for specimen scale, brush finish and upper-left diffuse lighting. Both references are style only. Use a gently elevated three-quarter view. Keep a clear silhouette and restrained fine detail that reads at 96–112 px. Center the subject with generous clear margins on every side. Deliver square PNG artwork at least 1024 × 1024 with genuine transparent alpha outside the fruit. Give it a broad, round, subtly squat profile and a short natural woody stem seated in a shallow depression. Keep the shoulder broad and the neck absent. Use the Bosc reference for russet brushwork and finish. Paint the Hosui's own golden-brown, matte russet skin with honey and tan washes, muted bronze shadow, and numerous small, irregular pale lenticels visible across the fruit.

## Cranberries

**Provisional ID** `provisional-cranberries-thumbnail-v1`

**Verified anatomy** Fresh American cranberries (*Vaccinium macrocarpon*) are round and red when ripe. UMass field photos show scarlet through burgundy fruit with small dark blossom-end markings visible on some berries. [Virginia Tech Dendrology](https://dendro.cnre.vt.edu/dendrology/syllabus/factsheet.cfm?ID=694) and [UMass Cranberry Station fruit-stage photographs](https://www.umass.edu/agriculture-food-environment/sites/ag.umass.edu/files/frost-tolerance-reports/frost_tolerance_report_9.14.22.pdf).

**Staged style reference** [blueberries-thumbnail-v1.png](../02-tomatoes-early-produce/blueberries-thumbnail-v1.png).

**Prompt**

> Create one Orchard produce thumbnail in naturalistic botanical watercolor. Image 1 is Orchard’s illustration-language sheet. Image 2 is a staged Orchard asset that guides framing, brushwork and visual weight only. Paint the requested fruit with a gently elevated three-quarter view and soft upper-left light. Build form with broad translucent overlapping washes, subtle granulation, selective surface detail and occasional softly broken edges. Center a compact composition with generous clear margins on a square PNG canvas at least 1024 × 1024 px. Keep true transparent alpha around the fruit. The thumbnail should read at 96–112 px. Omit paper patches, painted checkerboards, halos, cast shadows, props, lettering, borders, heavy outlines and hard glossy reflections. Paint five whole ripe American cranberries as a tight, naturally overlapping mound, with gently varied berry shapes and orientations, smoothly rounded silhouettes and red skins from bright scarlet to deep claret. Only one or two berries show a tiny, recessed dark blossom-end scar. The blueberries in the style reference guide arrangement and paint finish only. Do not copy their large open crowns or powdery bloom. Use small warm pink reserved highlights for the smooth fresh skin. Omit leaves and stems.

**Check status** [Candidate PNG](provisional-cranberries-thumbnail-v1.png) generated with the built-in tool. `checkMaster` passed on the 1254 × 1254 transparent PNG. Pass at 104 px on ivory and warm charcoal. The compact cluster of red berries, rounded forms and small blossom-end scars read clearly. Approved by Jared.

## Dates

**Provisional ID** `provisional-dates-thumbnail-v1`

**Verified anatomy** Fully ripe Medjool date fruit (*Phoenix dactylifera*) is plump and broadly oblong to ovate, with brown skin and irregular wrinkles. [FAO cultivar description](https://www.fao.org/4/y4360e/y4360e05.htm) and [OHSU Moore Institute photograph](https://www.ohsu.edu/sites/default/files/2025-09/Lesson%201%20Nutrition%20Card%20Set.pdf).

**Staged style reference** [figs-thumbnail-v1.png](../06-mangoes-fall-fruit/figs-thumbnail-v1.png).

**Prompt**

> Create one Orchard produce thumbnail in naturalistic botanical watercolor. Image 1 is Orchard’s illustration-language sheet. Image 2 is a staged Orchard asset that guides framing, brushwork and visual weight only. Paint the requested fruit with a gently elevated three-quarter view and soft upper-left light. Build form with broad translucent overlapping washes, subtle granulation, selective surface detail and occasional softly broken edges. Center a compact composition with generous clear margins on a square PNG canvas at least 1024 × 1024 px. Keep true transparent alpha around the fruit. The thumbnail should read at 96–112 px. Omit paper patches, painted checkerboards, halos, cast shadows, props, lettering, borders, heavy outlines and hard glossy reflections. Paint three whole fully ripe Medjool dates in a compact overlapping cluster with distinct oblong silhouettes, differing diagonal angles and rounded ends. The fruit should look plump under thin irregularly wrinkled skins. Paint with warm chestnut to deep reddish-brown washes, a few soft amber highlights along raised folds and darker brown in the creases. Vary the wrinkles in length and direction and keep the skins supple in appearance. Use the fig reference for overlapping watercolor forms only. Omit stems, foliage, split fruit and exposed pits.

**Check status** [Candidate PNG](provisional-dates-thumbnail-v1.png) generated with the built-in tool. `checkMaster` passed on the 1254 × 1254 transparent PNG. Pass at 104 px on ivory and warm charcoal. The plump oblong forms and reddish-brown wrinkled skin read clearly. Visual note: the wrinkle highlights are pronounced at master scale. Approved by Jared.
