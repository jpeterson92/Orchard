# Brassicas · Step 1

Three first-pass future-catalog candidates for Jared’s review. All three names are provisional because there were no matching records under `content/items/`. The batch checked the manifest, canonical requests, live masters, public exports and staged art for duplicates before generating.

The [Orchard illustration guide](../../../docs/design/illustration-guide.md#2-reference-free-prompt-pack) and the broccoli study on the [illustration board](../../../docs/design/references/04-illustration-language.png) guided the painting. Each subject used one built-in image generation call with transparency enabled. Each staged file is a 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNG and passed [`checkMaster`](../../../scripts/art-lib.ts). The visual check used 72 px cards and 320 px item-pane previews on Orchard ivory and warm charcoal. All three silhouettes read distinctly, with no conspicuous pale fringe at those sizes.

## Broccoli

Proposed asset ID and staged PNG [`provisional-broccoli-thumbnail-v1`](provisional-broccoli-thumbnail-v1.png). Image checks passed. Pending Jared’s review.

A broad crown of tight unopened green buds sits on short branches that join a single trimmed stalk. The visible leaf joins the stalk below the crown. The painting fills slightly more of the square than the other two, which Jared may want to compare at card size. [Illinois Extension’s broccoli guide](https://extension.illinois.edu/gardening/broccoli) and [University of Minnesota Extension’s broccoli harvest guidance and foliage reference](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/growing-broccoli).

**Image generation prompt**

> Paint one isolated grocery produce specimen in the Orchard editorial botanical watercolor style. Use only the lower-right broccoli study on the attached board as a reference for paint handling, not plant structure, background, or composition. Use luminous translucent layered washes, subtle pigment granulation and pooling, selective fine marks, mostly clean softly broken edges, and soft diffuse upper-left light. Warm contemporary illustrated produce journal. Gently elevated three-quarter view; centered and fully visible with about 12% clear margin on every side in a 1024 × 1024 square. Read clearly at 48–64 px. Genuine transparent alpha, including all space around the specimen; no paper, checkerboard, ground wash, cast shadow, text, border, props, shiny rendering, or heavy outlines.
>
> Paint a single trimmed supermarket heading broccoli stalk with one broad, gently domed green crown. Use several closely packed, gently distinct lobes of tight tiny unopened green flower buds. Show sturdy short green branches that physically connect those lobes to one thick light green main stalk with a plausible trimmed base. Keep branches mostly tucked beneath the dense crown. Include one small restrained blue-green leaf on a short petiole visibly meeting the lower stalk. No leaves among the bud clusters, yellow flowers, detached florets, or secondary specimen. Keep the dominant silhouette a wide crown over one short stalk.

## Cauliflower

Proposed asset ID and staged PNG [`provisional-cauliflower-thumbnail-v1`](provisional-cauliflower-thumbnail-v1.png). Image checks passed. Pending Jared’s review.

A dense ivory curd forms a continuous dome. The leafy wrappers cup it from below, with the visible ribs meeting the short basal stem. The green base and modeled curd preserve the silhouette on ivory. [University of Minnesota Extension’s cauliflower guide](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/growing-cauliflower) and [Illinois Extension’s exhibiting guide](https://extension.illinois.edu/sites/default/files/exhibiting_vegetables_0.pdf).

Use only the lower-right broccoli study on the attached board as a reference for paint handling, not plant structure, background, or composition. Use luminous translucent layered washes, subtle pigment granulation and pooling, selective fine marks, mostly clean softly broken edges, and soft diffuse upper-left light. Read clearly at 48–64 px. Genuine transparent alpha, including all space around the specimen; no paper, checkerboard, ground wash, cast shadow, text, border, props, shiny rendering, or heavy outlines.
>
> Paint one whole trimmed white cauliflower with a dense, low-domed creamy-ivory curd. Model tightly merged rounded lobes with fine restrained stipple in cream, warm ivory and muted cool shadow. Let the curd read as one compact continuous mound. Show four modest attached protective leaves in muted blue-green with pale ribs; they rise from around the short basal stem and cup the lower sides, leaving most of the curd visible. Keep the green leaves connected at the base and never scattered on top. No tall exposed stalk, open flowers, or separated florets. Keep the curd dimensional and legible against ivory.

## Green cabbage

Proposed asset ID and staged PNG [`provisional-cabbage-thumbnail-v1`](provisional-cabbage-thumbnail-v1.png). Image checks passed. Pending Jared’s review.

Broad, smooth overlapping leaves form the whole globe. The outer wrappers curl closely around the head, with the visible front ribs converging toward the trimmed base. [Illinois Extension’s cabbage guide](https://extension.illinois.edu/gardening/cabbage) and [Illinois Extension’s exhibiting guide](https://extension.illinois.edu/sites/default/files/exhibiting_vegetables_0.pdf).

**Image generation prompt**

> Paint one isolated grocery produce specimen in the Orchard editorial botanical watercolor style. Use only the lower-right broccoli study on the attached board as a reference for paint handling, not plant structure, background, or composition. Use luminous translucent layered washes, subtle pigment granulation and pooling, selective fine marks, mostly clean softly broken edges, and soft diffuse upper-left light. Warm contemporary illustrated produce journal. Gently elevated three-quarter view; centered and fully visible with about 12% clear margin on every side in a 1024 × 1024 square. Read clearly at 48–64 px. Genuine transparent alpha, including all space around the specimen; no paper, checkerboard, ground wash, cast shadow, text, border, props, shiny rendering, or heavy outlines.
>
> Paint one whole trimmed smooth green heading cabbage as a solid round, slightly squat globe. Shape the entire head with broad, smooth cupped green leaves wrapping and overlapping toward the top, with one or two attached outer wrappers hugging the lower globe. Each visible main rib rises from the short basal core and branches naturally across its own leaf. Use muted blue-green outer folds and lighter yellow-green where upper leaves catch light. Keep edges soft and gently curved; the silhouette stays compact and closed. No exposed curd, florets, long stalk, loose rosette, heavily blistered Savoy texture, or detached leaves.
