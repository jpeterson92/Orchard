## Ginger

Proposed asset ID `provisional-ginger-thumbnail-v1` (revision 1, item record pending).

**Subject reference** · [Texas A&M AgriLife Extension](https://agrilifeextension.tamu.edu/library/gardening/ginger/) describes the rhizome and its cream-to-light-brown skin. [University of Wisconsin Extension](https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/ginger-zingiber-officinale/) describes the pale yellow interior.

**Subject prompt** · Append to the shared prompt.

Let the silhouette read as a joined, irregular branching rhizome with no foliage, separate garnish slices or bright orange flesh.

### Yuca

Proposed asset ID `provisional-yuca-thumbnail-v1` (revision 1, item record pending).

**Alt text** · Tapered yuca root with rough brown skin and a cut end showing white flesh.

**Subject reference** · [UF/IFAS Extension’s cassava reference](https://gardeningsolutions.ifas.ufl.edu/plants/edibles/vegetables/cassava/) shows the brown-skinned root and its exposed white flesh.

**Style references** · The staged sweet potato sets root-family scale and paint handling. The staged ginger sets the treatment of an attached fresh cut face. [First reference](../15-roots-tubers/provisional-sweet-potatoes-thumbnail-v1.png) and [second reference](./provisional-ginger-thumbnail-v1.png).

**Subject prompt** · Append the reference roles and this description to the shared prompt.

> Paint one market-trimmed cassava root (yuca) diagonally from lower left to upper right. Give it a long, sturdy, slightly uneven cylindrical body with a slow taper to a blunt distal tip. The near thick end is cut across to reveal a small oval of dense, even, starchy white flesh, edged by a narrow rind. Paint the exterior earthy medium brown with rough bark-like texture, short longitudinal fissures and sporadic horizontal scars. Lift the upper and lower edges with restrained warm reflected light so the brown silhouette reads on charcoal. Avoid leafy yucca, a swollen sweet potato shape, orange flesh, branching fingers, separate slices or side roots.

**Check status** · [Staged PNG](provisional-yuca-thumbnail-v1.png). `checkMaster` passed at 1254 × 1254 px with genuine alpha. The long tapered form, rough brown rind and white cut end read on both backgrounds. Visible span is 81.8%.

### Coconuts

Proposed asset ID `provisional-coconuts-thumbnail-v1` (revision 1, item record pending).

**Alt text** · A brown fibrous coconut with three eyes beside a half showing white flesh around a hollow center.

**Subject reference** · [UF/IFAS Extension’s coconut palm reference](https://gardeningsolutions.ifas.ufl.edu/plants/trees-and-shrubs/palms-and-cycads/coconut-palm/) describes the mature brown hairy shell, three eyes and white meat.

**Style references** · The staged avocado sets handling for dark, rough texture. The approved Orchard mango master sets the tropical-family scale and translucent modeling. [First reference](../11-tropical-staples/provisional-avocados-thumbnail-v1.png) and [second reference](../../masters/mangoes-thumbnail-v1.png).

**Subject prompt** · Append the reference roles and this description to the shared prompt.

> Paint a mature, husked grocery coconut. Show one whole round-oval brown nut and one closely overlapping cut half in front, both with the same brown shell and a short fringe of residual tawny coir. Tilt the whole nut to show three small recessed eyes grouped at one end, with short fibers following the shell. Tilt the half to expose a firm, smooth white ring of meat against the thin dark shell and a visibly recessed hollow center, pale gray-white inside. Keep fibers short and selectively rendered, with enough midtone and reflected light for the brown silhouette on charcoal. Avoid green outer husk, an opaque solid white disk, pooled visible liquid, foliage and scattered fragments.

**Check status** · [Staged PNG](provisional-coconuts-thumbnail-v1.png). `checkMaster` passed at 1254 × 1254 px with genuine alpha. ### Dragon fruit

Proposed asset ID `provisional-dragon-fruit-thumbnail-v1` (revision 1, item record pending).

**Alt text** · Rosy-pink dragon fruit with green-tipped scales beside a cut half of white flesh dotted with black seeds.

**Subject reference** · [UF/IFAS Extension’s dragon fruit guide](https://ask.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/hs303) describes the oblong scaled fruit and small embedded black seeds. [UC Davis Postharvest Research and Extension Center](https://postharvest.ucdavis.edu/produce-facts-sheets/dragon-fruit) documents the white-fleshed red-skinned type.

**Style references** · The staged papaya sets the weight and overlap of whole fruit and cut companion. The approved Orchard mango master sets tropical-family framing and wash depth. [First reference](../11-tropical-staples/provisional-papaya-thumbnail-v1.png) and [second reference](../../masters/mangoes-thumbnail-v1.png).

**Subject prompt** · Append the reference roles and this description to the shared prompt.

> Paint the red-skinned, white-fleshed market type of dragon fruit. Show one whole oblong rosy-magenta fruit and a closely overlapping longitudinal half angled toward the viewer. Several elongated tapered scales arise from the skin, pink at the base and soft yellow-green toward their lifted tips. Let a few break the rounded silhouette naturally. The cut half has a thin magenta peel surrounding evenly white flesh with numerous tiny irregularly spaced black seeds embedded throughout. Avoid a central seed cavity, citrus segments, red flesh, evenly drawn polka dots, spines, neon coloring or foliage.

**Check status** · [Staged PNG](provisional-dragon-fruit-thumbnail-v1.png). `checkMaster` passed at 1254 × 1254 px with genuine alpha. The rosy skin, green-tipped scales and black seeds in white flesh read on both backgrounds. **Touch-up** · The pair spans 87.8% of the canvas. Reduce its scale toward the 75–85% family target.
