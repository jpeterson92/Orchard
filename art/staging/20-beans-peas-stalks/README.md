## Green beans

- Proposed asset ID `provisional-green-beans-thumbnail-v1`.
- Proposed item slug `green-beans`.
- Alt text. Long, slender green bean pods with a few finer haricots verts.
- Identifying traits. Slender round-podded green snap beans have a long, nearly even profile with a fine seam, a short stem and a tapered tip. Young seeds barely interrupt their outline. [Extension reference](https://ag.purdue.edu/department/hla/extension/extension-publications-library/ext-pubs/ho-175-w.html)
- Check status. `checkMaster` passed for the 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNG. Painted span is 83.8%. The long pods and finer haricots verts read at card size on both backgrounds.
- First-pass review note. The thickest pods are fuller than the haricots verts. Jared approved this candidate as drawn.

> Create a naturalistic botanical watercolor for Orchard’s produce-shopping catalog. Use the attached approved sweet corn only as a reference for luminous layered washes, selective pigment granulation, clean silhouette, softly varied edges, scale, and diffuse upper-left light. Paint the specified subject in a gently elevated three-quarter view, with warm highlights and fresh green shadows that show over ivory and warm charcoal. Center a compact composition spanning 75–85% of the square at its longest canvas axis, with clear space on all sides. Keep the shape readable at 40 and 72 px, with observed detail at 320 px. Deliver a square PNG at least 1024 px on genuine alpha transparency. No paper, background, halo, cast shadow, text, border, glossy rendering, or heavy outlines. Paint a compact, slightly fanned cluster of five whole, long, narrow green bean pods, including two finer haricots verts. Show softly curved, near-cylindrical bodies with largely smooth sides, faint longitudinal seams, tiny clipped stems and tapering tips. Paint varied natural leaf greens and lightly overlap the pods diagonally. Keep them closed with no conspicuous bead-like bumps, broad pea-pod faces, greenery, or cut pieces.
