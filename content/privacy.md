Orchard works without an account, and it never asks for, works out or stores where you are.

- **Search stays on your phone.** What you type is matched on the device and never sent anywhere.
- **The offline copy stays on your phone.** Orchard saves its pages so they work without signal. Clearing the site's data removes them.
- **A planning date lasts until you close the app.** It's kept on the device, never sent.
- **Speed is measured in totals only.** When you leave a page, your phone sends how long it took to show the first verdicts and to respond to taps. Orchard adds them to a daily count. No page, item or search goes with them, and nothing identifies your visit.
- **Use is counted the same way.** Your phone also sends how many times you opened an item, opened a card on Today or searched for something we don't cover, and whether you came back within two weeks. Your phone remembers the date of your last visit for that and never sends it. What you searched for and which items you opened are never sent.
- **No ad trackers and no selling of data.**
- **No IP addresses stored.** Orchard keeps no record of who visits. The site is served by Cloudflare, which handles each request under [its own privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
