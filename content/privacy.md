Orchard works without an account, and it never asks for, works out or stores where you are.

- **Search stays on your phone.** What you type is matched on the device and never sent anywhere.
- **The offline copy stays on your phone.** Orchard saves its pages so they work without signal. Clearing the site's data removes them.
- **Feedback is anonymous.** A tap of "At your store today" sends the item, the verdict you saw, your answer, the label you picked if any, and the app version. We store it with the date only: no time, no IP address, nothing about where you are. A tap made without signal waits on your phone until the connection returns, and your phone remembers today's answer so the page can show it.
- **"Bought it" stays on your phone.** On a later visit Orchard asks "How was it?" once, and sends your answer with the item, the verdict you saw when you bought it, the label you picked if any, and the app version. We store it with the date only. Unanswered questions are forgotten after 14 days.
- **A planning date lasts until you close the app.** It's kept on the device, never sent.
- **No ad trackers, no analytics and no selling of data.**
- **No IP addresses stored.** Orchard keeps no record of who visits. To stop floods of feedback, the server counts taps for a minute against a scrambled form of the connection's address, which is never stored. The site is served by Cloudflare, which handles each request under [its own privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
